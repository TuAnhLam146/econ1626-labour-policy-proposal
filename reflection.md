# AI Use Reflection

I used ChatGPT throughout this assessment mainly for technical support, planning, and editing. It helped me understand how to use GitHub and Markdown, including structuring the repository, formatting tables, and creating navigation links. I also used it to analyse the assessment instructions and HD rubric, which helped me develop a clear structure before deeper research. This made it easier to identify what evidence I needed for each section rather than researching AI and labour markets too broadly. As an international student, I also used ChatGPT to check grammar, coherence, and whether my arguments were expressed clearly in English. However, I did not accept AI output automatically. For example, when ChatGPT suggested a structure for the policy brief, I used it as a starting point but changed the order and emphasis of several sections after comparing it with the rubric and my research. I kept the structure only where it helped me address the marking criteria while retaining my own argument and policy direction. A major limitation was that editing assistance could sometimes change more than my grammar. ChatGPT occasionally reframed my original idea or altered the my argument direction while making it sound more polished. I therefore had to reread every revision carefully and compare it with my original reasoning and sources to ensure the final argument remained my own.
