# Policy Proposal - Assignment 2
## ECON1626 by Chris 

## Project Overview

This repository contains a policy proposal for ECON1626. The proposal examines the effects of generative AI on the Australian labour market and advises the Department of Employment and Workplace Relations on policy responses to uneven AI adoption, workforce adjustment, and productivity challenges.
